For me the game of the year 2025 is Dead Island 2 for multiple reasons.
Although this game released in 2023, I only played it this year and I enjoyed it thoroughly. I did not want to launch the game and move away from the main menu as the main menu itself was so beautiful and the music was elite. They could not have picked a better main menu theme even if they tried again [Drown by Karen O, Danger Mouse](https://open.spotify.com/track/5NiKdj69Uqqe34wgtj8h9T) embodies the vibe of this game perfectly.
